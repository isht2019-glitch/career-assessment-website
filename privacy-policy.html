Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 18, 2025

TheApp ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our career assessment application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

We collect the following personal information when you use our App:

  • Email Address: Used for account creation, authentication, and payment verification
  • Name: Used to personalize your experience
  • Test Results: Your personality assessment answers, aptitude test scores, and career recommendations

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you use the App, we may automatically collect:

  • Device information (device type, operating system version)
  • App usage data (features used, time spent in the app)
  • Log data (IP address, access times, app crashes)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Provide Services: To deliver career assessment results and personalized recommendations
  • Account Management: To create and manage your user account
  • Payment Processing: To verify payment status and grant access to premium features
  • Improve Our App: To analyze usage patterns and improve user experience
  • Communication: To send important updates about your account or our services
  • Security: To protect against fraud and unauthorized access

3. Data Storage and Security

We use Firebase (Google Cloud Platform) to store and manage your data securely. Your information is:

  • Encrypted in transit using SSL/TLS protocols
  • Stored in secure, industry-standard data centers
  • Protected by Firebase Authentication and security rules
  • Backed up regularly to prevent data loss

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: With Firebase/Google Cloud for hosting and authentication services
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or government regulation
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets (users will be notified)

5. Your Data Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Delete your account and all associated data at any time through the app settings
  • Data Portability: Request your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent for data processing (may limit app functionality)

6. Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time through the app settings. When you delete your account:

  • All your personal information will be permanently deleted
  • Your test results and career recommendations will be removed
  • Your payment approval status will be revoked
  • This action cannot be undone

7. Children's Privacy

Our App is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us, and we will delete such information.

8. Third-Party Services

Our App uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Authentication: For secure user authentication
  • Firebase Firestore: For data storage and synchronization
  • Google Analytics (if applicable): For app usage analytics

These services have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them:

  • Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

9. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you services. If you delete your account, we will delete your data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal purposes.

10. Cross-Platform Synchronization

Our App supports cross-platform synchronization between web and mobile versions. Your data is synchronized across platforms to provide a seamless experience. You can access your test results and account information from any platform where you're logged in.

11. Cookies and Tracking

Our web version uses local storage and session storage to:

  • Keep you logged in
  • Remember your preferences
  • Store test progress temporarily

You can clear this data through your browser settings at any time.

12. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence. These countries may have different data protection laws. By using our App, you consent to such transfers.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:

  • Posting the new Privacy Policy on this page
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date
  • Sending you a notification through the App (for significant changes)

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

By using our App, you consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.
